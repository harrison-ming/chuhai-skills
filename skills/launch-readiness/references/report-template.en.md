# Report template (English)

Readers are non-technical cross-border sellers. `audit.py` already wrote a draft from this template (`data/report.draft.md`); your job is to review and edit it, not to start from scratch.

## Writing rules (for the agent)

- Conclusion first, short plain sentences; no evidence-level codes (E1-E6) or technical words in the body (see `scripts/check_report.py`); technical details go to the appendix.
- Badges exactly: `[Verified]` `[Store claims]` `[Third-party]` `[Shown on page]` `[Inferred]` (with "Basis: ..." on the same line) `[Conflicting]` `[Not found]`. Label low rather than high.
- **Separate facts from legal judgements**: "The return policy says 7 days [Verified]" is a fact; "differs from the official EU 14-day withdrawal requirement [Inferred] Basis: EU Consumer Rights Directive (source S5)" is a judgement.
- **Never state a legal conclusion** (the self-check blocks those words); write "matches / differs from the official requirement of X" with `[Inferred]`.
- `[Conflicting]` must quote both texts; drop it if, after re-reading, they are about different things (e.g. warranty vs return window).
- Paste mode: facts are `[Store claims]` ("source: text you provided"); on-page checks are `[Not found]`.
- Blocked or timed-out pages are `[Not found]`, never "missing".
- The appendix states `Status: complete` (or partial / failed), same as `summary.json`.

## Fixed ending (append after the appendix, keep verbatim)

```markdown
---

### What this report does not cover

- Needs a browser: whether the cookie banner really appears and what is sent before consent, cart and checkout pages, mobile layout.
- Needs your admin: Merchant Center settings, payment activation, tax registration, real delivery times.
- This report compares public pages (or text you provided) with official guidance retrieved on the check date. **It is not legal advice.** For legal obligations (EU/UK/Canada/Australia consumer law, privacy law, product safety) ask a local lawyer or a professional adviser before launch.

Full multi-market checks, browser tests, re-check comparison: https://github.com/harrison-ming/chuhai-skills#pro

<sub>Generated by launch-readiness (chuhai-skills)</sub>
```
