# Report template (English)

The reader is a non-technical cross-border seller. `radar.py diff --lang en` writes `data/diff.md`, already laid out section by section with badges. Your job: copy it to `data/report.md`, write "Signals to watch" and smooth the wording. Do not change numbers, remove badges, or drop the appendix and the fixed ending.

## Writing rules (for the agent)

- Conclusion first, short plain sentences; prices with currency, shares in percent, dates as YYYY-MM-DD.
- No technical words in the body (data endpoint names, field names, file names, evidence level codes); technical details go to the appendix. `scripts/check_report.py` holds the full list.
- Badges exactly: `[Verified]` (difference between two direct reads of public store data) `[Shown on page]` (homepage, theme, app traces, tracking codes, country sites; and anything from the web archive) `[Store claims]` (policy text content) `[Inferred]` (followed by "Basis: ...", for every interpretation of yours) `[Conflicting]` (two sources disagree) `[Not found]` (not obtained this time). `[Third-party]` is rarely needed here.
- A change happened at some point between the two captures: **never name the exact day**, write "between A and B".
- No sales, profit or ad spend claims; prices are public list prices in the base currency, not checkout prices per country; an app trace is not proof of use; a payment icon is not proof of an enabled method.
- Only write about removals when diff.md shows a removal table; when it says removals are not judged, do not write "N products were removed".
- "Signals to watch": 3-5 items, each an action (tonight / this week / watch) + event ids + `[Inferred] Basis: ...`.
- The appendix states `Status: complete` (or partial / failed) as in diff.md; demo reports keep the "Demo data, not a real store" line.

## Template (change report)

```markdown
# <Store> competitor change report

<sub><domain> · compared <old date> to <new date> · <N> days apart</sub>

## At a glance

- Period: <old date> to <new date>. <where each capture came from>
- This period: <a> new, <r or not judged> removed, <d> price cuts, <u> price rises, <s> discounts started [Verified]
- Most important changes:
  1. <change> <badge> (<event id>)
- Next suggested run: <date>. Tell the assistant "check <domain> for changes again".

## Price changes
<Store-wide change if any; table of products changing ≥ 5%; "N more small changes, see price-changes.csv".>

## New and removed products
<New products; removals or why they are not judged; sold out / back in stock; new options; launch pace.>

## Promotions
<Discounts started / ended / deeper; share on sale before and after; homepage promo text before and after [Shown on page].>

## Tech and market changes
<Theme, app traces, tracking codes, currency, country sites, shipping countries.>

## Policy changes
<Per policy page: key terms before and after [Store claims]; up to 3 added / removed sentences, the rest in the appendix.>

## Signals to watch
- This week: <action> (<event id>) [Inferred] Basis: <what the change suggests>

## What we could not check
<Dimensions not comparable, failed parts, archive limits. Missing data is not the same as no change.>

## Appendix: method and sources
- Status: <complete/partial/failed>
<Snapshot table, method and thresholds, raw warnings, event ids, technical terms.>
```

A baseline report (only one snapshot) is titled "<Store> competitor baseline report" with sections At a glance, Current products and prices, Tech and markets today, Policy key terms, Signals to watch, What we could not check, Appendix. Status `partial`; when the store is unreachable, blocked or not Shopify it is a short `failed` report, still delivered.

## Fixed ending (after the appendix, keep as is)

```markdown
---

### What this report does not cover

- Back-and-forth changes between the two captures: we only see two points in time, so a price that dropped and rose again in between is invisible.
- Things only a browser shows: cart and checkout offers, pop-ups, member prices, real prices per country, ad campaigns.
- Things only the merchant's admin knows: real sales, stock levels, conversion, profit, promotion results.
- This report is based on public information only and is not due diligence or legal advice.

Watch several competitor stores at once, with history charts and scheduled checks: https://github.com/harrison-ming/chuhai-skills#进阶

<sub>Generated by competitor-change-radar (chuhai-skills)</sub>
```
