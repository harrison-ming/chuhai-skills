# Report template (English)

Readers are sellers without a technical background. `compute.py --lang en` already writes `data/report-draft.md` from this template; your job is to review and edit it, not to start over.

## Writing rules (for the agent)

- Conclusion first, short plain sentences; amounts with currency, shares as percentages.
- No evidence codes (E1-E6) or technical words in the body (full list in `scripts/check_report.py`); file names and field names belong in the appendix.
- Label every load-bearing claim exactly: `[Verified]` `[Store claims]` `[Third-party]` `[Shown on page]` `[Inferred]` (followed by "Basis: ...") `[Conflicting]` `[Not found]` `[You provided]`. Label low rather than high.
- **Icons and scripts are `[Shown on page]` only**; never write that a method is enabled unless the seller sent a screenshot.
- No overall score, no approval prediction, no "guaranteed", no provider ranking, no referral links.
- Priorities may be lowered with a reason, never raised into "will be rejected / frozen".
- The appendix states `Status: complete` (or partial / failed), matching `status` in `summary.json`.

## Fixed ending (after the appendix, unchanged)

```markdown
---

### What this report does not cover

- Things only your back office shows: whether a payment provider is really enabled, review and risk status, your contract rates, holds and chargeback history.
- Things only visible inside checkout: which payment methods the checkout really offers, availability per market, whether a payment succeeds. This tool never enters checkout and never places orders.
- Tax (sales tax, VAT), company formation, foreign exchange and cross-border fund compliance: out of scope; ask a licensed professional.
- Based on public pages and your answers, a snapshot of one day; not financial, tax or legal advice. Account opening, reviews and risk decisions are made by the providers; this report guarantees none of them and makes no promise about business results or income. Rates follow the providers' official pages and your contract.

Multi-store checks, browser tests of pre-checkout pages, fee comparison of several setups: https://github.com/harrison-ming/chuhai-skills#进阶

<sub>Generated by payments-readiness (chuhai-skills)</sub>
```
